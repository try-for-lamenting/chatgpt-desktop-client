Help & Usage
Tabs

Click or press Ctrl+T to open a new tab. Each tab runs an isolated browser session.

Switch tabs with Ctrl+Tab / Ctrl+Shift+Tab. Close with Ctrl+W. Drag to reorder. Press Ctrl+R or click to reload.

Adding Accounts

Each saved account is a snapshot of your login cookies. To add one:

  1. Open the Account Switcher via Alt+A or the button.
  2. Click Clear Cookies to sign out the current tab.
  3. Sign into ChatGPT with the account you want to save.
  4. Type a name (e.g. Work) and click Save.
  5. Repeat from step 2 for each additional account.
Switching Accounts

In the Account Switcher each saved account shows two icon buttons:

Load with Context — restores cookies, grabs a share link of the current chat, and reopens it under the new account.

Delete — permanently removes the saved account.

Drag the grip handle on each row to reorder. Use Import / Export to back up as JSON.

Companion Window

Press ⊞+Alt+/ to toggle the floating Companion — a slim overlay that stays on top for quick queries.

Inside the companion, Alt+A opens the account panel, starts a new chat, and moves the session to a main tab.

Sharing & Cookies

Copy Share Link grabs a public URL for the active conversation and copies it to your clipboard.

Clear Cookies signs out the current tab without affecting other tabs or saved accounts.

Customize shortcuts via the settings button.
